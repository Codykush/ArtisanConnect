Our Journey

From a Simple Idea to a Movement

ArtisanConnect was born in 2025 from a simple observation: talented artisans were struggling to reach customers beyond their local communities, while people were searching for authentic, handmade products.

ArtisanConnect Headquarters - Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh

What started as a small initiative to help local artisans in Greater Noida has grown into a nationwide movement supporting hundreds of artisans across India. We believe every handmade piece tells a story — of tradition, skill, and the artisan's dedication.

Based in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, our platform brings these stories to you, creating meaningful connections between makers and appreciators while preserving cultural heritage for future generations.

Our Leader

The Visionary Behind ArtisanConnect

Meet the driving force behind our mission to preserve traditional crafts

Founder & CEO

Kushagra Singh Bhadauria

Cultural Preservation Advocate & Social Entrepreneur

"We're not just building a platform; we're preserving heritage, empowering communities, and creating a legacy of craftsmanship for future generations."

Based in Greater Noida, Uttar Pradesh, Kushagra brings over a decade of experience in cultural preservation and sustainable development. His passion for traditional crafts stems from growing up surrounded by artisans and witnessing their struggles firsthand. With degrees in Social Entrepreneurship and Cultural Studies, he combines business acumen with cultural sensitivity to create meaningful impact.
